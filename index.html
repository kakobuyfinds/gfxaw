From product link to parcel

  1. Confirm the seller listing

    Open the CAFANS product page and check its agent options. If you have the original marketplace link, paste it into your SuperBuy account and confirm the item and variant load before paying.

  2. Compare measurements and photos

    For shoes, compare the insole length in centimeters. For hoodies and tees, measure a garment you own across the chest. For jackets, check sleeve length and ask for a close-up of the zipper if the warehouse photos leave it unclear.

  3. Get a shipping quote

    Use your destination and packed parcel dimensions to compare available routes. Check the billable weight, tracking, and any separately quoted charges before selecting a route.

SuperBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping list organized around product links, prices, and variants. Use this independent guide to shortlist CAFANS listings and confirm individual items with your shopping agent.

Do the listed prices include international shipping?

The catalog shows estimated item prices in USD. Add the current domestic delivery charge, international freight quote, and any other applicable costs when you budget.

What should I do if the seller link or size is unavailable?

Reopen the original listing and check the exact size and color combination. If the seller has removed the variant, use the full CAFANS category to find another listing and verify its measurements before ordering.
